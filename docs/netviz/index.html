What is NetViz?
NetViz is a live topology + telemetry view of the PeerCompute network. The center cube is your local node; amber cubes are peers and blue-white icosahedrons are relays. Amber links show direct peer connections, green spurs show relayed hops, and blue-white arcs show pubsub traffic. Pulses indicate message direction along each link. Click any node or edge to inspect detailed stats.
