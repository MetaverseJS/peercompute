NO IMAGERY LAYER. The globe is terrain geometry plus elevation contours.

The default NOAA ETOPO 2022 source is true pole-to-pole relief and bathymetry at about 1.85 km north-south native resolution at the equator. It is a global relief model for visualization, not navigation.

The optional AWS source combines SRTM, GMTED, ETOPO1, and regional inputs for better land detail, but its Web Mercator terrain stops at ±85.051°.
