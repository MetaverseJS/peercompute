# Multiscale Ladder Branch

## Goal
Build the keystone PeerCompute demo that lets users zoom from supergalactic structure down to galaxies, solar systems, planetary weather, human-scale physics, MLS-MPM material patches, molecular dynamics, and Schrodinger-style orbital clouds.

## Current Direction
- Continuous zoom remains the long-term UX target, but the immediate implementation priority is bottom-up physics: Schrodinger/orbital closure -> molecular dynamics and chemistry -> source/sink accounting -> material/SPH/MPM coupling -> larger scales.
- Bottom-up means the layer below must provide the contract consumed by the layer above. The Schrodinger/orbital layer should publish condition-aware atom/molecule material properties and behavior surfaces first; MD should not hide missing lower-layer physics with constraint fixes that look like chemistry.
- A law graph is the emerging simulation framing: a bipartite graph where state variables are one type of node, laws/constraints are another type of node, and simulation is the process of finding a consistent update across the whole network.
- Future architecture should likely treat "layers" as view/resolution metadata over a dependency graph of law/domain workers. Some domains are more scale-invariant than others, and not every scenario needs every intermediate scale computed. Defer that refactor until the Schrodinger/material foundation is stronger.

## Accuracy Boundary
The first demo is an integrated runtime scaffold. It is not yet a scientific supergalactic-to-quantum simulator.

Scientific claims require:
- named solver per layer;
- units/dimensions on every exchanged field;
- conservation audits;
- validation data and tolerances;
- validity domains and uncertainty;
- deterministic hashes/provenance;
- clear interactive vs scientific execution mode.
- Current Schrodinger material potential is a reference-table plus screened-hydrogenic descriptor contract with WebGPU-only qgrid/radial and qmat worker paths, a qmat law-graph fragment, a packet-level law-graph consistency/readiness/update-plan report, a reduced graph fixed-point solve report, proxy proposal/result admission reports, a scheduler-facing dispatch queue/manifest, a state-application preflight report, a WebGPU-only ULG reduced state-delta worker, a WebGPU MD source-term consumer for that reduced delta, a WebGPU MD quantum source-term consumer for orbital/material coupling, and a WebGPU MD qmat source-term consumer for reduced material force/property/electronic/barrier/product-topology batches with an interactive existing-atom product-group mutation path. It does not yet provide calibrated Born-Oppenheimer force gradients, calibrated reactive charge transfer, authoritative molecular/material worker-buffer mutation, a validated nonlinear scientific law-graph solver, or an energy-conserving product/stoichiometry solver for Na-water chemistry.
